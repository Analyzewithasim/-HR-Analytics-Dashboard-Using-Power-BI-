# 🎯 HR Analytics Dashboard Using Power BI 📊
This project centers on an HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to help HR professionals understand and mitigate employee attrition. The dashboard provides actionable insights into employee demographics, attrition rates, and the impact of factors like job satisfaction, department, and work-life balance on turnover.

![Alt Text](https://github.com/Analyzewithasim/-HR-Analytics-Dashboard-Using-Power-BI-/blob/main/HR%20Analytics.gif)


💡 Purpose
To enable HR teams to make data-driven retention decisions by visualizing critical metrics that contribute to employee attrition. This project aims to provide HR departments with insights into workforce trends, identify high-risk groups, and highlight areas for policy improvement.

🔍 Key Visualizations
Page 1: Employee Demographics
- Stacked Column & Bar Charts: Show employee distribution by age, gender, education field, and job role.
- Donut Charts: Visualize department size and business travel, key indicators for demographic insights.

Page 2: Attrition Analysis
- Pie Chart: Attrition by department, showing which departments face higher turnover.
- Matrix: Displays job satisfaction ratings, linking satisfaction to attrition rates.
- Stacked Column Chart: Attrition by marital status and overtime, offering insights into the impact of personal factors and workload.
  
Page 3: Financial & Workload Factors
- Line and Stacked Column Chart: Monthly income by job level, analyzing income influence on retention.
- Stacked Bar Chart: Attrition by job satisfaction and work-life balance, illustrating retention factors at a glance.
- Line Chart: Attrition by distance from home, providing insights into commuting's effect on turnover.
  
📈 Key Results & Insights
- Departmental Attrition Patterns: High attrition rates in specific departments and roles, signaling potential areas for retention efforts.
- Impact of Work-Life Balance: Correlation between overtime and higher attrition, supporting policies that improve work-life balance.
- Commuting Factors: Higher attrition among employees with long commutes, suggesting remote work options could help retention.
  
🛠️ Skills Demonstrated
- Data Visualization: Built detailed, user-friendly dashboards using Power BI, including various charts and filters.
- Data Preparation: Cleaned and transformed data using SQL and Excel to create structured datasets ready for analysis.
- KPI Development: Defined metrics such as attrition rate, average employee age, and job satisfaction to drive insights.
- Data Storytelling: Presented findings in a concise, accessible manner, enabling HR teams to quickly understand complex data relationships.
